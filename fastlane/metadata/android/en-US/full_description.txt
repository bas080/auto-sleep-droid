Auto Sleep Droid is a simple Android sleep timer for media playback.

Fall asleep peacefully to your favorite podcasts, YouTube playlists, audiobooks, or music without waking up hours later to blaring audio or unexpected autoplay videos.

Features:
- Controlled entirely from the notification shade.
- Compatible with YouTube, Spotify, podcast players, and most audio/video apps using system media controls.
- Fades volume down gently before pausing playback.
- Screen-free countdown reset using physical volume buttons.
- Automatically listens for media playback.
- Open source with no trackers or analytics.
