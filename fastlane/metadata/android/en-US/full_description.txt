Puts your phone to sleep when you fall asleep, wakes you up when you're ready.

Listen to your favorite podcasts, audiobooks, music, or videos without playing audio all night or waking up to autoplay.

Features:
- Controlled directly from the main screen or notification shade.
- Quick Nap Alarm feature to easily set or cancel nap timers.
- Smart Wake-Up Goal safeguard to protect minimum sleep duration.
- Compatible with YouTube, Spotify, podcast players, and most audio/video apps using system media controls.
- Fades volume down gently before pausing playback.
- Screen-free countdown reset using physical volume buttons or phone flip gesture.
- Automatically listens for media playback.
- Open source with no trackers or analytics.
