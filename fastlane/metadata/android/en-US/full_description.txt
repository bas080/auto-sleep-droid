Auto Sleep Droid is a simple Android sleep timer for media playback.

It works entirely from the notification shade and requires no separate app screen.

Features:
- Controlled entirely via ongoing notifications.
- Volume-button press resets active countdown.
- Fades volume before pausing media playback.
- Automatically listens for media playback.
- Open source with no trackers or analytics.

Release notes: https://github.com/bas080/auto-sleep-droid/releases
