Auto Sleep Droid Manual

Auto Sleep Droid is a low-friction sleep timer and wake-up safeguard designed to control media playback without requiring screen interaction in bed.

Sleep Timer

  • Set Duration: Tap Sleep 20m (or Set Timer) in the notification shade to set duration from 1 minute to 24 hours.
  • Automatic Playback Detection: The timer sits waiting until media starts playing, then automatically begins counting down.
  • Fade & Pause: When timer expires, audio slowly fades down over 30 seconds before media playback is paused, and original volume is restored.
  • Screen-Free Resets: Press any physical volume button or flip your phone over while in bed to reset the timer to full duration without looking at your screen.

Smart Wake-Up Goal

  • Set Goal: Tap Set Goal (or Alarm HH:MM) in the notification shade to set target wake-up time and minimum required sleep duration.
  • Automatic Alarm: Schedules a system wake-up alarm for your target goal time (or sets a new alarm when dismissed). If sleep timer activity occurs within 12 hours prior to your goal time, the alarm is pushed later to guarantee your minimum sleep duration.
  • Gentle Crescendo Wake-Up: When the wake-up alarm triggers, the tone volume gradually increases over 3 minutes along a gentle psychoacoustic curve for a peaceful transition.
  • Screen-Free Alarm Controls:
    • Snooze (9m): Flip your phone over or tap Snooze in the notification shade.
    • Dismiss: Press a physical volume button or tap Dismiss in the notification shade.

Import & Export Settings

  • Export: Tap Export in the app header to share your current configuration.
  • Import: Tap Import in the app header to paste and restore configuration JSON strings.
