Auto Sleep Droid Manual

Auto Sleep Droid puts your phone to sleep when you fall asleep, and wakes you up when you're ready. It is a low-friction sleep timer and wake-up safeguard designed to control media playback with main screen configuration and intuitive notification controls.

Main Configuration Screen

Settings are logically grouped under Timer, Alarm, Health Connect, Do Not Disturb, and Backup headings.

  • Sleep Timer: Enable or disable the sleep timer and set the countdown duration by tapping the duration button to open a duration wheel dialog. Timer duration controls remain enabled when the sleep timer switch is OFF.
  • Target Wake-Up Goal: Enable daily wake-up alarms, set your target wake-up time, view/edit your current wake-up time, and configure minimum sleep duration safeguards. The wake alarm operates independently of the sleep timer switch.
  • Health Connect Sync: Toggle Health Connect integration under the Health Connect section to automatically persist sleep and wake times to Android Health Connect upon wake. Toggling ON opens Health Connect permission settings, while toggling OFF revokes Health Connect permissions.
  • Do Not Disturb: The Sync with Do Not Disturb toggle enables automated DND tracking so the sleep timer automatically turns ON when DND is active and turns OFF when DND ends.
  • Backup Section: Tap Export settings to share your current configuration JSON string via Android's system share menu. Tap Import settings to paste, validate, and restore configuration JSON strings.
  • About Section: Tap Version to check for release updates on GitHub. Tap Feedback to send feedback or report issues via email (prompting whether to include event logs). Access Manual, Logs, and Donate from Links. Full-screen views for Manual and Event Logs feature a Back button in the bottom-right corner.

Sleep Timer & Notifications

  • Notification Controls: The ongoing notification shade display provides a single-tap toggle action (Disable / Enable). Notification content displays text-colored symbols for fade-out time (♪) and wake alarm time (⏰︎). Before going to sleep and until your alarm is dismissed, the notification title displays Click notification when awake. Clicking the notification registers I'm Awake, stops active alarms, and updates your wake schedule without opening the main UI. Once clicked, the notification title reverts to Auto Sleep Droid and subsequent clicks open the main UI.
  • Automatic Playback Detection: The timer sits waiting until media starts playing, then automatically begins counting down.
  • Fade & Pause: When timer expires, audio slowly fades down over 30 seconds before media playback is paused, and original volume is restored.
  • Screen-Free Resets: Press any physical volume button while in bed to reset the timer to full duration without looking at your screen.

Smart Wake-Up Goal

  • Independent Wake Alarm & Current Wake Time: The wake alarm operates independently of whether the sleep timer is turned on. You can adjust the upcoming wake alarm clock time using the Current wake-up time setting. When you go to bed early, the sleep timer automatically moves your wake alarm earlier. Sleep timer resets push the current wake time forward when needed to enforce your minimum sleep duration safeguard.
  • Gentle Crescendo Wake-Up: When the wake-up alarm triggers, the tone volume gradually increases over 3 minutes along a gentle psychoacoustic curve for a peaceful transition.
  • Snooze Alarm (9m): Press any physical volume button when the wake alarm triggers to snooze for 9 minutes.
  • Stop Alarm & Early Wake-Up ("I'm Awake"): Clicking the notification during your awake window (or when an alarm is ringing or snoozed) is the sole action to stop a ringing or snoozed alarm. It sets your current wake-up time to 15 minutes before current time (capped at your target goal time), registers your sleep session in Health Connect (if enabled), and reschedules your alarm for tomorrow.

Health Connect Integration

  • Automatic Sleep Logging: When Health Connect sync is enabled, Auto Sleep Droid automatically records your sleep and wake times as sleep sessions in Android Health Connect upon alarm dismissal. Night sleep start time uses when your sleep timer originally started, falling back to your minimum sleep duration if no timer was run.
  • Min Session Duration Threshold: Configure a minimum session threshold (default 15 minutes) under the Health Connect section to filter out short accidental wake-ups from being saved to Health Connect.
  • Permissions & Data Control: Toggling Health Connect ON opens Android Health Connect permission settings to grant permission. Toggling OFF revokes Health Connect permissions, stopping future sync while preserving previously recorded sleep data.

Import & Export Settings

  • Export: Tap Export settings under Backup to share your current configuration string via Android's system share menu.
  • Import: Tap Import settings under Backup to paste, validate, and restore configuration JSON strings.
  • Feedback: Tap Feedback under About to send an email with feedback directly to the developer, with an option to include event logs.
