Auto Sleep Droid Manual

Auto Sleep Droid is a low-friction sleep timer and wake-up safeguard designed to control media playback with main screen configuration and intuitive notification controls.

Main Configuration Screen

  • Sleep Timer: Enable or disable the sleep timer and set the countdown duration directly from the main screen.
  • Target Wake-Up Goal: Enable daily wake-up alarms, set your target wake-up time, and configure minimum sleep duration safeguards.
  • Show Notification: Toggle whether the ongoing sleep timer notification is shown in the notification shade.
  • Logs: Tap Logs in the action link header to open a dedicated screen displaying real-time system event logs.

Sleep Timer & Notifications

  • Notification Controls: The notification shade display provides an easy single-tap Disable or Turn On action.
  • Automatic Playback Detection: The timer sits waiting until media starts playing, then automatically begins counting down.
  • Fade & Pause: When timer expires, audio slowly fades down over 30 seconds before media playback is paused, and original volume is restored.
  • Screen-Free Resets: Press any physical volume button or flip your phone over while in bed to reset the timer to full duration without looking at your screen.

Smart Wake-Up Goal

  • Daily Recurring Alarm: The wake-up alarm is set daily. When the current alarm rings, the app automatically schedules the alarm for the next day at the same goal time, while protecting your sleep duration if you sleep late.
  • Gentle Crescendo Wake-Up: When the wake-up alarm triggers, the tone volume gradually increases over 3 minutes along a gentle psychoacoustic curve for a peaceful transition.
  • Screen-Free Alarm Controls:
    • Snooze (9m): Flip your phone over or tap Snooze on the alarm notification.
    • Dismiss: Press a physical volume button or tap Dismiss on the alarm notification.

Import & Export Settings

  • Export: Tap Export in the app header to share your current configuration.
  • Import: Tap Import in the app header to paste and restore configuration JSON strings.
