Auto Sleep Droid Manual

Auto Sleep Droid puts your phone to sleep when you fall asleep, and wakes you up when you're ready. It is a low-friction sleep timer and wake-up safeguard designed to control media playback with main screen configuration and intuitive notification controls.

Main Configuration Screen

Settings are logically grouped under Nap, Timer, Alarm, and Health Connect headings.

  • Nap Alarm: Tap Nap on the main screen to launch a quick nap duration dialog and start a nap alarm. If a nap is already running, tapping Cancel Nap cancels the active nap alarm.
  • Sleep Timer: Enable or disable the sleep timer and set the countdown duration by tapping the duration button to open a duration wheel dialog. Timer duration controls remain enabled when the sleep timer switch is OFF.
  • Auto Sleep Timer (DND): Toggle ON to open Android Do Not Disturb settings. When enabled, the sleep timer automatically turns ON when Do Not Disturb is active and turns OFF when DND ends. The sleep timer can always be toggled manually regardless of this setting.
  • Target Wake-Up Goal: Enable daily wake-up alarms, set your target wake-up time, view/edit your current wake-up time, configure minimum sleep duration safeguards, and tap I'm Awake if you wake up before your alarm to record your sleep session and cancel today's alarm. The wake alarm operates independently of the sleep timer switch.
  • Health Connect Sync: Toggle Health Connect integration under the Health Connect section to automatically persist sleep, nap, and wake times to Android Health Connect upon wake or nap alarm dismissal. Toggling ON opens Health Connect permission settings, while toggling OFF revokes Health Connect permissions.
  • About Section: Tap Version to check for release updates on GitHub. Access Manual, Logs, Feedback, Donate, Export, and Import from Links. Full-screen views for Manual and Event Logs feature a Back button in the bottom-right corner.

Sleep Timer & Notifications

  • Notification Controls: The ongoing notification shade display provides single-tap toggle actions (Disable / Enable) and a quick Nap / Cancel Nap button. All notification content is concise and directly visible.
  • Nap Timer: Tap Nap on the main screen or in the notification shade to set a quick nap duration and start a nap alarm. If a nap is active, tapping Cancel Nap cancels the nap. Resetting the sleep timer pushes any active nap alarm forward by the same reset increment. Dismissing a nap alarm does not affect your current wake-up time.
  • Automatic Playback Detection: The timer sits waiting until media starts playing, then automatically begins counting down.
  • Fade & Pause: When timer expires, audio slowly fades down over 30 seconds before media playback is paused, and original volume is restored.
  • Screen-Free Resets: Press any physical volume button or flip your phone over while in bed to reset the timer to full duration without looking at your screen.

Smart Wake-Up Goal

  • Independent Wake Alarm & Current Wake Time: The wake alarm operates independently of whether the sleep timer is turned on. You can adjust the upcoming wake alarm clock time using the Current wake-up time setting. On alarm trigger or dismiss, the next wake-up time steps back towards your target goal time by 15 minutes. Sleep timer resets automatically push the current wake time forward to enforce your minimum sleep duration safeguard.
  • Gentle Crescendo Wake-Up: When the wake-up alarm triggers, the tone volume gradually increases over 3 minutes along a gentle psychoacoustic curve for a peaceful transition.
  • Snooze Alarm (9m): Flip your phone over or tap Snooze on the alarm notification when the alarm triggers to snooze for 9 minutes.
  • Dismiss Alarm: Press a physical volume button or tap Dismiss on the alarm notification to dismiss the wake alarm.
  • I'm Awake: If you wake up before your alarm, tap I'm Awake on the main screen or in the notification shade. This registers your sleep session in Health Connect, cancels today's pending wake alarm, steps your wake-up time 15 minutes back towards your goal time, and reschedules your alarm for tomorrow.

Health Connect Integration

  • Automatic Sleep & Nap Logging: When Health Connect sync is enabled, Auto Sleep Droid automatically records your sleep, nap, and wake times as sleep sessions in Android Health Connect upon alarm dismissal.
  • Permissions & Data Control: Toggling Health Connect ON opens Android Health Connect permission settings to grant permission. Toggling OFF revokes Health Connect permissions, stopping future sync while preserving previously recorded sleep data.

Import & Export Settings

  • Export: Tap Export in the bottom links list to share your current configuration string via Android's system share menu.
  • Import: Tap Import in the bottom links list to paste, validate, and restore configuration JSON strings.
  • Feedback: Tap Feedback in the Links dialog to send an email with feedback directly to the developer.
